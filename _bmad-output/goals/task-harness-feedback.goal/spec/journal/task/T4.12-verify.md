  footer's own figure and its `room` are not proven (three mutants survive).
- Empty / Null ("a session that is not a /task root is unchanged"): proven for `runOf`, `footerText` and the sidebar;
  not for the prompt footer (the `own` mutant removes today's footer for every session and the suite stays green).

## Fix that would close the gap

1. Move the footer's inputs into `usage.ts` as pure functions, for example `ownFigure(usage)` (today's join) and
   `footerRoom(width, hint)`, and test each one on its output (with and without a cost; 80, 120 and 160 cells).
2. Pin the rest of the footer wiring in the source check: the guard `if (!props.sessionID) return Option.none<string>()`,
   the calls of the new functions, and the Match body `{text()}`; keep the existing two pins.
3. Add `prompt/index.tsx` mutants (the five above) to `journal/gate/t4.12-mutants.py` and show them caught.

This follows the T4.10 precedent: a route checked by its source text is accepted when the check pins the exact
guard and body and the logic it calls is tested on its output.
