## Per test case

1. Happy Path — 40 seeds x 100 random facts fold without a throw, including facts after `done` and unknown ids —
   PARTLY COVERED (gap).
   - Covered: `slicesOf` folds 100 generated facts per seed through `apply`; `apply` dies on an `error` snapshot
     (index.ts:82-84), so "no throw" also means no machine error. Unknown ids `T7`, `T7.c1` and `S99` are in the
     generator's pools (lines 82, 135, 140); with 6 tasks in the plan, `T7` alone is drawn for about 3 % of facts.
     No assertion proves that any of them occurs (minor).
   - Gap: `slicesOf` (lines 183-199) cuts the slice at the fact that makes the run `done` and folds the next fact
     onto a fresh `initial(plan)`. No random fact is folded onto a `done` snapshot. The only fact after `done` is
     one fixed `{ type: "run.resumed", at: 1000 }`, applied through `apply` alone (line 236). AD-6 also defines a
     slice as the facts from one `run.started` to the next, so late facts before the next `run.started` belong to
     the ended slice; the test never builds that case.
2. Concurrency — `apply` equals a live actor sent the same facts — COVERED for facts before `done`; GAP after.
   `createActor(machine)` in `Effect.scoped`, `start`, `send` each fact, `getSnapshot`; `shape(live)` toEqual
   `shape(folded)` (status, value, whole context, log included). An external `send` waits for its macrostep
   (port Actor.ts:245-249), so the mailbox is drained. The live actor never receives a fact after `done`, which
   AD-5 test (1) ("including facts after `done`, both by `apply` and by `send` to a live actor, and compare") and
   RM5 ("including facts after the run ended ... also sent to a live actor") require. The port drops such sends
   without failing (Actor.ts:248-249), so the test can send them.
3. State Transitions — prefix restored, suffix sent, equal to the full fold, log included — COVERED.
   `machine.getPersistedSnapshot(half)`, `createActor(machine, { snapshot })`, send `slice.slice(prefix)`,
